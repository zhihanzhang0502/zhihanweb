# Quantitative Research Internship

## Overview

During my internship at Evolution Labs, I worked on futures-market signal research and quantitative strategy evaluation.

My work included processing multi-contract futures data, evaluating anonymized factor signals, engineering time-series features, comparing forecasting models, translating model outputs into cross-sectional long-short positions, and analyzing strategy performance under different ranking and smoothing rules.

The project used Python-based data-processing, machine-learning, and backtesting workflows. Because the underlying market data and factor definitions are proprietary, this page presents only the research framework and high-level findings.

**Role:** Quantitative Research Intern (Strategy Focus)  
**Organization:** Evolution Labs, Shanghai, China  
**Duration:** June 2025 – August 2025
