# E-commerce Consumer Credit Model

This project studies when e-commerce platforms should launch consumer credit services, such as buy-now-pay-later, and whether such services should be shared with third-party sellers.

## Project Overview

The project develops a stylized mathematical model to analyze platform credit-service decisions. It studies the conditions under which launching consumer credit services improves platform outcomes.
