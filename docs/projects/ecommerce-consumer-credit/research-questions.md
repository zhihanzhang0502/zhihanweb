# Research Questions

This project addresses the following questions:

- Under what conditions should a hybrid e-commerce platform launch a consumer credit service?
- When should the platform share its consumer credit service with a third-party seller?
- How do consumer preference, repayment risk, commission rates, and service fees affect the platform's and seller's strategies?
- How does the introduction of consumer credit affect retail prices, product demand, firm profits, and consumer surplus?
- Are consumer credit services still beneficial when some consumers choose not to use them?
