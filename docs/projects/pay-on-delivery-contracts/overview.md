# Pay-on-Delivery and Distribution Contracts

## Overview

This project studies how pay-on-delivery services interact with distribution-contract choices in a dual-channel supply chain.

The model considers a manufacturer that sells through an offline channel and cooperates with an e-commerce platform to establish an online channel. The online channel may operate under either a wholesale contract or an agency contract. Pay-on-delivery can reduce the online channel's disadvantage caused by delivery delay, but it also postpones firms' cash recovery and creates opportunity costs.

My contributions included developing the game-theoretic supply chain model, conducting equilibrium and comparative analyses across payment and contract scenarios, performing Mathematica-based numerical analysis, and contributing to the co-authored manuscript.
