# Research Questions

This project addresses the following questions:

- Does DEX net trade flow predict short-horizon returns on centralized exchanges?
- Do current and lagged DEX trading flows contain different information about subsequent CEX price movements?
- Does Ethereum network congestion strengthen the relationship between DEX net flow and CEX returns?
- Does CEX illiquidity weaken the transmission of information from DEXs to CEXs?
- Are the results robust to trading-volume controls, token-pair fixed effects, and date fixed effects?
