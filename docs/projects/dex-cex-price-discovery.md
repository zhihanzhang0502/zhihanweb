# DEX-CEX Price Discovery

This project studies information flow and price discovery between decentralized exchanges and centralized exchanges in cryptocurrency markets.

The research uses high-frequency trading data from decentralized exchanges and centralized exchanges to analyze whether price movements in one market lead or predict movements in another market.

## Main Tasks

- Collected and processed high-frequency cryptocurrency trading data.
- Constructed panel datasets for DEX and CEX markets.
- Designed liquidity-related and flow-based variables.
- Estimated regression models to study price discovery and market interaction.

## Skills Used

- Python
- R
- Data cleaning
- Panel data construction
- Regression analysis
- Cryptocurrency market microstructure
