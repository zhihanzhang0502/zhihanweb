## Main Findings

- AI-assisted tools can improve content creativity and production efficiency, but they may also increase content homogeneity.
- The platform should introduce AI tools when creator efficiency is low, AI-induced homogeneity is limited, or creators have strong creative ability.
- The optimal AI assistance intensity is not necessarily the level that maximizes content quality, because the platform must balance quality improvement, homogeneity risk, and development cost.
- When AI tools are introduced, content quality, consumer browsing volume, creator profit, and consumer surplus can increase under the model's adoption conditions.
- The effect of creator efficiency is nuanced: after AI introduction, higher creator efficiency may not always improve creator profit or consumer surplus.
- Creator creativity consistently improves content quality and benefits the platform, creators, and consumers.
