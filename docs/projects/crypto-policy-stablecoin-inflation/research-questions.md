# Research Questions

## Main Research Question

How is stablecoin-driven digital dollarization associated with inflation across countries?

## Specific Questions

1. Is lagged stablecoin exposure associated with year-over-year CPI inflation after controlling for domestic macroeconomic conditions and fixed effects?

2. Does crypto-policy restriction intensity change the relationship between stablecoin exposure and inflation?

3. Does the estimated relationship differ across Legal, Restricted, and Illegal crypto-policy regimes?

4. Do exchange-rate depreciation and external-balance vulnerability help explain the relationship between stablecoin exposure and inflation?

5. Does the main stablecoin-exposure coefficient remain stable after adding alternative fiscal, monetary, and macro-financial interaction terms?
