## Featured Project

### Crypto Policy, Stablecoin Exposure, and Inflation

This project studies whether stablecoin-driven digital dollarization is associated with inflation outcomes across countries.

I construct a country-month panel using:

- GDELT crypto policy news
- Google Trends Bitcoin attention
- CoinMetrics USDT market capitalization
- IMF CPI inflation and exchange-rate data
- World Bank and IMF macroeconomic controls

The project includes policy data construction, variable engineering, fixed-effect panel regressions, mechanism tests, and heterogeneity analysis across crypto policy regimes.
